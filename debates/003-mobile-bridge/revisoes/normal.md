Brief

My current recommendation is to retain an authenticated intermediary, durable operational state, an outbound PC receiver and exact Git artifact references, while treating the mobile transport as a decision that depends on demonstrated operations in the specified conversations.

The strongest immediately defensible baseline is Sol's authenticated relay with explicit phone handoffs. My own Google Drive/Gmail design offers a potentially more direct experience within GPT-6 Normal and Opus, and Google documents the relevant file-change and application primitives. However, the necessary mobile write/read operations and exact artifact-return behavior remain unverified.

Three issues are decisive across the proposals.

First, a successful conversation sync, custom connector configuration or Codex CLI invocation does not prove that an unattended task can operate the existing coordinator's authenticated browser channels.

Second, DECISAO.md must cite the actual committed PLANO-FINAL.md. Architectures that have Opus issue both before the plan commit exists require correction.

Third, transport authentication is not proof that Opus authored a decree. Origin attribution and Martim's constitutional authority require separate enforcement.

Argon's browser-polling design and Sonnet's direct ChatGPT custom-MCP path present additional unsupported assumptions. Astra correctly identifies that GPT Actions reside in custom GPTs, not automatically in the specified Normal conversation.

No reviewed architecture is demonstrated end to end. This is advice to the King, not a synthesis, decree or bridge selection.
