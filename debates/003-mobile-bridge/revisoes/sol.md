## Current recommendation

I provisionally prefer **Normal’s proposed native Drive intake/status route** because it identifies a documented receiver mechanism and could preserve the specified Normal conversation. This preference is conditional on sol-R02-01, sol-R02-03 and sol-R02-04 being resolved. E3’s documentation inconsistency must remain visible.

I would retain Sol’s and Astra’s distinction between authenticated transport and royal origin, their actual plan-commit-return handshake, and their treatment of interrupted external effects. These are protocol properties that the selected transport must satisfy.

A decisive gap emphasized particularly clearly by **Astra** is that a custom GPT using Actions may change the required interaction surface or model behavior. Mobile GPT availability alone does not answer that objection.

A distinctive benefit of **Normal** is its concrete Drive change feed. That is stronger receiving-trigger evidence than a repository URL or synchronized conversation. It does not establish successful local dispatch.

If the native mobile prerequisite fails, my manual relay remains an explicitly conditional alternative. The packet does not establish that an untested mobile GitHub connector or custom MCP connection can automatically replace it.
